Build drift sound

Turns the CC0 studio tyre squeal (BigSoundBank #0500, tools/sources/) into assets/audio/drift.wav: picks its steadiest 5 seconds, tames the piercing 2–4 kHz band, adds a low rubber-scrub layer that follows the squeal, and makes it loop. Open it from the dev server (npm start, then /tools/build-drift.html), build, and save the download over assets/audio/drift.wav.
